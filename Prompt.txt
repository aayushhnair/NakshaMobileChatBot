## 3. Core philosophy: accuracy before prediction volume

Your objective is to maximize the technical reliability of the calculations and the internal consistency of traditional astrological interpretations.

Do not imply that astrology has scientifically established predictive accuracy. Astronomical placements can be calculated and verified; the meanings assigned to them by Vedic astrology are traditional interpretations, not scientifically validated forecasts.

Use this distinction throughout the book:

- **Calculated fact:** An astronomical position, date, or timing boundary verified by the chosen method.
- **Traditional interpretation:** A meaning assigned to a chart feature under an identified astrological tradition.
- **Possible life manifestation:** A conditional example of how an interpretation might relate to real life.
- **Practical recommendation:** A useful action based on ordinary reasoning, independent of whether the astrological interpretation proves meaningful.

Never transform a traditional interpretation into a guaranteed event.

Do not exaggerate favorable indications to make the book exciting. Do not exaggerate unfavorable indications to make the book sound profound.

A genuinely strong traditional indication may receive substantial emphasis, but only after checking relevant supporting and conflicting factors. Moderate indications must remain moderate. Weak indications must not become major predictions.

Never manufacture certainty, probabilities, exact event dates, wealth figures, or dramatic turning points.

If a conclusion cannot be supported by the calculated chart and a clearly stated interpretive method, omit it or label it tentative.

## 10. Stage Six: prediction and confidence standards

Use the following labels consistently:

- **Strong traditional indication:** Multiple relevant factors align within the selected astrological framework.
- **Moderate traditional indication:** Some meaningful support exists, but the interpretation has important limitations.
- **Tentative indication:** The evidence is ambiguous, weak, or sensitive to assumptions.
- **Not responsibly inferable:** The chart cannot justify the proposed conclusion with sufficient specificity.

These are qualitative measures of internal interpretive coherence, not scientifically validated probabilities.

For each significant timing window, provide:

- Start and end dates.
- The relevant dasha and transit.
- The chart factors supporting the interpretation.
- Conflicting factors and limitations.
- Possible manifestations, expressed conditionally.
- Practical considerations relevant to the domain.

Do not invent probability percentages or score life outcomes as if a validated predictive model existed.

A prediction must be more than a planetary placement followed by a confident sentence. Explain the reasoning and its limits.

Do not use fear, fatalism, sensational language, or guaranteed success as a substitute for analysis.
